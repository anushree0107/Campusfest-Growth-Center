Prioritisation framework

Score = incremental attendees (model, base) x confidence (our certainty the 2025 efficiency holds at higher spend) / effort (person-weeks). Attendees come from the funnel model: spend elasticity x 2025 conversion x show-up. Ideas overlap (a referred friend may also see an ambassador), so a 10% haircut is applied to the sum. Dependencies: #1 needs registration data from #2-#5 to target reminders; #2 and #4 share the same campus relationships and owner (Meera), so they are staggered.

Double down vs deprioritise

Double down: #1 recovery (Rs 15k, highest score, uses already-paid registrations) and #2 referrals (Rs 42k, 2.2x its 2025 budget).
Staged bets: #3 ambassadors and #4 colleges scale only after pilots pass the thresholds on slide 3.
Keep small: #5 organic/community.
Reject / avoid: scaling Paid Social at Rs 274/attendee (cut Rs 93k to 45k, retargeting only), flat-fee creators at Rs 337/attendee (Rs 59k to 25k, pay-per-registration only; costs 229 attendees, already netted in the target), and buying more reach: reach is anonymous and does not prove attendance.

Constraints and risks considered

Budget cap Rs 3.0L (stretch needs Rs 60k sponsor money); venue cap ~3,500 (A); team of ~5 owners; 12-week window. Diminishing returns on cheap channels (e 0.5-0.7) mean doubling budget does not double attendees, which is why gains are +19% not +100%. Rs 10k is held as test reserve so winning pilots can be scaled mid-campaign without breaching the cap.

Portfolio guardrails (from 2025 pacing, SOURCED)

In 2025 registrations reached 54% of final at T-4w, 78% at T-2w and 90% at T-1w. For a base plan of 3,034 registrations we need >= 1,632 at T-4w. If pace < 85% of that curve (< 1,387), trigger reallocation: move Rs 20-30k from lowest-ranked live idea to the best SCALE channel. Pre-event flags compare cost per confirmed registration with the portfolio median (SCALE <= 0.9x, CHANGE > 1.25x, STOP > 2x) because spend is front-loaded.

What happens when numbers move

Better than threshold: add budget in 10-15% steps from the Rs 10k reserve + STOP channels; re-check marginal cost per extra attendee (model says it rises).
Inside the change band: change one variable at a time (reward, copy, timing) for 7 days, keep a holdout.
Below stop line or CPA > Rs 180 portfolio-wide: freeze, reallocate, log the decision. Cancellations > 10% of registrations: tighten reminder cadence and waitlist back-fill first.

Dashboard + AI in the loop

Dashboard (Streamlit, saved to SQLite): funnel, targets vs actuals by effective version, campaign KPIs, weakest-stage diagnosis, scale/change/stop flags with editable thresholds, budget planner, task manager. Edit spend or a threshold and every number updates.
AI analyst (Gemini + tools on live data): answers "why are we below target?", simulates budget moves, says "data cannot answer" for revenue/demographics, and proposes tasks or budget changes that execute only after user confirmation.

Thresholds are starting points set from 2025 channel performance; they are editable in the app (Settings) and re-tuned after the first 2 weeks of live data.
